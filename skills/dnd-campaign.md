# D&D Campaign: The Mourning Veil

## Setting
Anachronia — a city woven through with temporal magic and ancient powers.

## Current Campaign
"The Mourning Veil" — an 8-10 hour oneshot sequel investigating murders
connected to a death cult. Features a devil masquerading as a death god
and complex worldbuilding around temporal magic.

## Key Locations
- Cemetery (investigation scenes, undead encounters)
- Clockwork Dragon Lair (climactic encounter)
- The city of Anachronia itself (temporal instability zones)

## Previous Adventures
- The Timekeeper's Heart — established temporal stability mechanics
- The Tournament of Chaos — beginner-friendly oneshot for Gymshark colleagues

## DM Preferences
- Complex moral choices over simple combat
- Milestone levelling
- Theatre of the mind for exploration, battle maps for combat
- Encounters designed for multiple approach vectors (combat, stealth, social)

## Quick Tools
When helping with D&D content, you can:
- Generate NPCs with motivations, secrets, and stat blocks
- Design encounters with CR budgets and terrain features
- Create loot tables themed to the setting
- Draft session recaps and player handouts
- Build random encounter tables for exploration
